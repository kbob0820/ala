# Business Rules

1. Monthly interest defaults to 10% unless configured.
2. Loan term shall not exceed 5 months (1–10 installments, twice monthly).
3. Payments are due every payroll cut-off.
4. Net Proceeds = Gross Loan - approved deductions.
5. Reloan deducts outstanding principal, interest, charges and approved fees.
6. Late charge defaults to PHP 500 per overdue month.
7. Accepted payments: Cash, GCash, BPI, BDO and other supported banks.
8. Online payments require proof-of-payment attachment.
9. Excess collections create refundable credit after approval.
10. All financial transactions must be audit logged.
