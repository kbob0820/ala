# User Roles

## Super Admin
System configuration, security, users.

## Loan Officer
Borrowers, applications, approvals.

## Cashier
Receive payments, verify payment proofs.

## Collector
Record field collections and follow-ups.

## Finance
Refund approval, reconciliation, reports.

## Auditor
Read-only access to transactions and audit logs.

## Borrower Portal
View loans, schedules, balances, upload payment proof, download statements.
